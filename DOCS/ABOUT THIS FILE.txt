Article DOI: 10.1080/17583004.2026.2746370

PURPOSE AND COVERAGE
This workbook contains the corporate emissions and financial data, calculations, and analysis sheets associated with the article. It documents decarbonization–ROIC elasticity, transition quadrants, and decarbonization pace. The raw-data sheet contains 32,758 company-year records for 2018–2023. The final dataset contains 300 firms across 35 jurisdictions. The pace comparison includes 114 observed emissions-reducing firms.

WORKSHEET GUIDE
ABOUT THIS FILE: Article details, workbook overview, citation, and usage notes.
RAW DATA: Original company-year emissions records.
CLEANING PHASE I: First retained data-cleaning snapshot.
FINANCIALS: Emissions, Scope Integrated Emissions Footprint (SIEF), and ROIC.
ELASTICITY CALCULATION: Decarbonization–ROIC elasticity calculations.
CLEANING PHASE II: Further sample preparation and elapsed-year calculations.
FINAL DATA: Final firm-level elasticity dataset.
DESCRIPTIVES: Summary statistics and positive/negative elasticity counts.
PIVOT ANALYSIS: Pivot summaries, including elasticity by jurisdiction.
QUDRANT ANALYSIS: Emissions and ROIC changes, transition quadrants, and frequencies.
COMPANY SCATTER: Company-level data used for emissions/ROIC plots.
R1 PACE CALCULATION: Annualized emissions-reduction pace and ROIC changes.
R1 PACE TABLE: Summaries by pace group and supporting intervals.
R1 PEER COMPARISON: Pace groups and rankings for reducing firms.
R1 STATISTICAL TESTS: Association tests, group comparisons, and sensitivity statistics.
R1 PACE FIGURE: Data used for the annualized pace/ROIC figure.
R1 MATCHED WINDOW: Common-window and unannualized-change sensitivity data.

UNITS AND DATA CONVENTIONS
GHG emissions and SIEF are expressed in tonnes of CO2 equivalent (tCO2e). The inspected SIEF formulas sum Scope 1, market-based Scope 2, and Scope 3. ROIC is stored as a fraction in the financial and earlier/later ROIC fields. Pace is expressed in percent per year; annualized ROIC change is expressed in percentage points per year. Blank cells, the em dash, and "Information Not Available" indicate missing information and must not be interpreted as zero.

RETAINED LIMITATIONS
The supplied file retains four #DIV/0! cells in ELASTICITY CALCULATION and four #REF! cells affecting permutation-test p-values and their Monte Carlo standard errors in R1 STATISTICAL TESTS. Consult the repository README for exact cells and replication limitations.
